KEY IMPROVEMENT: The Prompt

OLD (Vague):
  Question alone → LLM thinks → Generic answer

NEW (Specific):
  Question + Document chunks + Explicit instructions → LLM answers based on doc

Example:
  Question: "What are the parking rules?"
  
  OLD: "Parking rules vary by location and municipality..."
  NEW: "The parking fee is Rs 500 per month with 2 spaces per flat..."

COMMON QUESTIONS:

Q: Why is first answer slow (10-30 seconds)?
A: Ollama loads 4.3GB model into memory. Later answers are 2-5 seconds.

Q: Why are answers still vague?
A: Check YOUR document - it might be vague. Use specific details and structure.

Q: Can I modify the RAG prompt?
A: YES! Search for "You are a helpful assistant" in rag_system.py

Q: Can I use different LLM?
A: YES! Change "mistral" to another model (llama2, neural-chat, etc)

Q: How to clear vector database?
A: Delete the chroma_db/ folder
