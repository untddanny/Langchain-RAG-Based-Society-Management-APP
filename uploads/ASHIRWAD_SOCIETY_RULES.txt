1. PARKING RULES AND REGULATIONS
========================================================

Rule 1.1: Parking Fee Structure
Implemented: 1st January 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- Monthly parking fee: Rs 500 per vehicle
- Annual parking fee: Rs 5,500 (if paid before 1st of every month)
- Late payment fine: Rs 50 per day (charged after 10th of every month)
- Each member is entitled to maximum 2 parking spaces
- First 2 vehicles get parking; additional vehicles need approval from management committee

Rule 1.2: Reserved Parking Spaces
Implemented: 15th March 2021 (Approved in 32nd AGM held on 28th February 2021)
- 5 reserved spaces for physically disabled residents (free of charge)
- 3 reserved spaces for senior citizens above 70 years (Rs 250/month)
- 4 reserved spaces for visitors and guests
- 2 reserved spaces for emergency and ambulance access
- Reserved spaces cannot be used for permanent parking

Rule 1.3: Visitor Parking
Implemented: 1st June 2020 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- Visitors can park in designated lot B (behind main gate)
- Visitor parking is free for first 4 hours
- Rs 50 per additional 2 hours
- Long-term visitor parking (more than 24 hours) requires written permission from Management Committee
- Overnight guest parking permitted from 8:00 PM to 8:00 AM (Rs 100 per night)

Rule 1.4: Parking Violation and Penalties
Implemented: 1st January 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- Unauthorized parking in reserved spaces: Rs 500 penalty + vehicle towing
- Parking in common areas (gardens, walkways): Rs 300 penalty + vehicle towing
- Blocking entrance/exit: Rs 1000 penalty + immediate towing
- Towing charges: Rs 2000 per occurrence (to be paid by vehicle owner)
- Repeat violations (3 times in 6 months): Show cause notice + potential membership action

Rule 1.5: Parking Allocation and Transfer
Implemented: 1st April 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- New members can apply for parking space allocation within 30 days of possession
- Parking spaces cannot be transferred without management approval
- Maximum waiting period for parking: 90 days
- Temporary parking can be arranged on monthly basis during waiting period (Rs 250/month)

2. MAINTENANCE AND COMMON CHARGES
========================================================

Rule 2.1: Monthly Maintenance Charge
Implemented: 1st January 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- Base maintenance charge: Rs 2000 per flat per month
- Water charges: Included in maintenance (up to 1000 liters per day)
- Electricity charges for common areas: Included in maintenance
- Cleaning charges: Included in maintenance
- Maintenance is due on or before 10th of every month
- Late payment fine: Rs 100 per day (from 11th onwards)
- Accumulated arrears: 15% interest per annum (compounded monthly)

Rule 2.2: Maintenance Fund Allocation (as of 2023)
Implemented: 15th April 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- Cleaning and housekeeping: 35% (Rs 700 per flat)
- Maintenance of common areas: 25% (Rs 500 per flat)
- Repairs and contingency: 20% (Rs 400 per flat)
- Staff salaries: 15% (Rs 300 per flat)
- Insurance and taxes: 5% (Rs 100 per flat)

Rule 2.3: Special Maintenance Charges
Implemented: 1st June 2021 (Approved in 32nd AGM held on 28th February 2021)
- Painting of common areas: One-time Rs 3000 per flat (when required, average every 5 years)
- Terrace waterproofing: One-time Rs 2000 per flat (when required, average every 7 years)
- Electrical system upgrade: One-time Rs 1500 per flat (as decided by AGM)
- Plumbing system repair: One-time Rs 1000 per flat (when required)
- These charges are collected only when needed and approved by 2/3rd majority in AGM

Rule 2.4: Exemptions and Concessions
Implemented: 20th August 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- Senior citizens (above 75 years): 25% discount on maintenance
- Physically disabled: 25% discount on maintenance
- Widows/Widowers: 15% discount on maintenance
- EWS members: 10% discount on maintenance
- These benefits are applicable on production of valid certificates

3. WATER AND ELECTRICITY CHARGES
========================================================

Rule 3.1: Water Consumption and Charges
Implemented: 1st January 2024 (Approved in 35th AGM held on 15th December 2023)
- Water supply: Daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM (regulated supply)
- Free quota: 1000 liters per day per flat (approximately 30,000 liters per month)
- Excess charges: Rs 50 per 1000 liters for consumption above quota
- Water meter reading: Taken on 1st of every month
- Bills generated: By 5th of every month
- Payment due: By 15th of every month

Rule 3.2: Electricity Charges for Common Areas
Implemented: 1st January 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- Common area electricity charge: Included in maintenance charge
- Includes: Staircase lights, corridor lights, gate lights, ground lights, pump house
- Load shedding schedule: During peak hours (12 PM to 6 PM), 2 hours per day
- Meter readings: Taken quarterly (1st of January, April, July, October)

Rule 3.3: Private Electricity
Implemented: 1st June 2020 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- Private electricity meters are individual responsibility
- DISCOM bills to be paid directly to electricity board
- Late payment to DISCOM: Risk of disconnection (individual responsibility)
- Society will charge Rs 500 fine for non-payment if board disconnects

4. SECURITY AND ACCESS CONTROL
========================================================

Rule 4.1: Security Staff and Arrangements
Implemented: 1st March 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- Security staff strength: 4 guards (2 day shift, 2 night shift)
- Working hours: Day shift 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Night shift 6:00 PM to 6:00 AM
- Gate opening hours: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM
- After 10:00 PM: Only residents allowed, visitors need permission from flat owner
- Emergency gate opening: Available 24/7 for medical emergencies

Rule 4.2: Visitor Pass and Registration
Implemented: 15th May 2021 (Approved in 32nd AGM held on 28th February 2021)
- All visitors must register at main gate
- Visitor pass valid for single day (issue time to 12:00 AM next day)
- For overnight guests: Written permission from resident required
- Long-term visitors (more than 7 days): Approval from Management Committee required
- Identity proof required at gate (Aadhar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport)

Rule 4.3: Vehicle Access and Sticker
Implemented: 1st January 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- All vehicles must display society sticker on windshield
- Sticker color: Red for members, Blue for registered guests
- Sticker replacement fee: Rs 100 per sticker (in case of loss/damage)
- No sticker vehicles not allowed entry (except visitors)
- Sticker issued by society office within 48 hours of registration

Rule 4.4: CCTV and Surveillance
Implemented: 15th August 2020 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- CCTV cameras installed at: Main gate, parking area, common pathways, terrace
- Video retention: 30 days
- Access to CCTV footage: Only Management Committee and official police requests
- Privacy policy: No access to individual flats or private spaces

5. COMPLAINT AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
========================================================

Rule 5.1: Complaint Registration Process
Implemented: 1st April 2021 (Approved in 32nd AGM held on 28th February 2021)
- Complaints must be filed in writing at society office
- Office hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Email: complaints@ashirwadsociety.com
- Phone: +91-9876543210 (Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM)
- Complaint registration: Within 24 hours
- Complaint reference number issued on same day

Rule 5.2: Complaint Categories and Resolution Time
Implemented: 1st April 2021 (Approved in 32nd AGM held on 28th February 2021)
- Water/Electricity emergency: Resolution within 4 hours
- Security/Safety issues: Resolution within 8 hours
- Maintenance issues: Resolution within 15 days
- Billing disputes: Resolution within 30 days
- Neighbor disputes: Resolution within 60 days (through arbitration)
- General complaints: Resolution within 45 days

Rule 5.3: Grievance Redressal Committee
Implemented: 15th February 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- Committee members: 5 (President, Vice-President, 3 elected members)
- Meets every week on Wednesday at 6:00 PM
- Unresolved complaints escalated to management committee
- If still unresolved, escalated to AGM in next meeting
- Members can appeal committee decision within 30 days

Rule 5.4: Anonymous Complaint Policy
Implemented: 1st June 2020 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- Anonymous complaints accepted for serious issues only
- Serious issues: Safety, security, unauthorized construction, fire hazard
- Anonymous complaints processed through Chairman only
- Confidentiality maintained for complainant identity
- Non-serious complaints require name and flat number

6. PET POLICY
========================================================

Rule 6.1: Pet Ownership and Registration
Implemented: 1st July 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- Pet ownership allowed with prior written permission from Management Committee
- Only dogs and cats permitted (no exotic animals, reptiles, birds)
- Maximum 2 pets per flat
- Pet registration fee: Rs 2000 per pet (one-time payment)
- Annual pet fee: Rs 500 per pet (due by March 31st every year)
- Pet vaccination certificate (rabies) mandatory every year

Rule 6.2: Pet Behavior and Rules
Implemented: 1st July 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- Dogs must be leashed when outside flat
- Pet owner responsible for any damage caused by pet
- Cleanup: Owner must clean pet waste immediately from common areas
- Vaccination: Mandatory annual rabies vaccination (certificate to be submitted to office)
- Aggression: Aggressive pets must be reported; repeated incidents lead to removal notice

Rule 6.3: Pet Violations and Penalties
Implemented: 1st July 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- Unleashed dog in common area: Rs 500 fine
- Not cleaning pet waste: Rs 300 fine
- Aggressive behavior: Show cause notice + warning
- Repeated violations (3 times in 6 months): Notice to remove pet within 30 days
- Unregistered pet: Rs 1000 fine + registration enforcement

7. NOISE AND DISTURBANCE RULES
========================================================

Rule 7.1: Quiet Hours
Implemented: 1st January 2021 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- Silent hours: 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM
- Loud music, TV, instruments: Not permitted during silent hours
- Construction/renovation: Only 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays (NO weekends/holidays)
- Vehicles idling: Maximum 5 minutes
- Honking: Prohibited except emergencies

Rule 7.2: Party and Gathering Rules
Implemented: 1st January 2021 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- Private parties: Allowed up to 10:00 PM
- Music system: Maximum 80 dB during parties
- Terrace events: Approval required from Management Committee
- Common hall booking: 3 days advance booking required
- Security deposit: Rs 5000 (refundable after event)
- Cleanup: Event organizer responsible for cleanup within 2 hours of event

Rule 7.3: Violations and Penalties
Implemented: 1st January 2021 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- First violation: Written warning
- Second violation: Rs 500 fine
- Third violation: Rs 1000 fine
- Fourth violation: Show cause notice
- Repeated violations: Potential disciplinary action by AGM

8. VISITING HOURS AND GUEST POLICY
========================================================

Rule 8.1: Visitor Guidelines
Implemented: 1st June 2020 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- Visitors allowed: 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM
- After 10:00 PM: Only registered overnight guests allowed
- Visitor registration at gate: Mandatory
- ID proof required: Aadhar, Voter ID, PAN, or Passport
- Entry denied without gate verification

Rule 8.2: Overnight Guest Policy
Implemented: 1st June 2020 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- Overnight guests: Allowed up to 7 days without notice
- Guests beyond 7 days: Written permission from Management Committee required
- Guest security deposit: Rs 5000 (for guests beyond 30 days)
- Permanent lodgers: Not allowed (violates apartment rules)
- Overnight guest pass: Valid only for registered guest

9. COMMON AREA USAGE RULES
========================================================

Rule 9.1: Garden and Landscape Areas
Implemented: 15th May 2021 (Approved in 32nd AGM held on 28th February 2021)
- Gardens are for all residents
- No plucking flowers or plants
- No dumping waste in garden areas
- No parking in garden area
- Gardening activities: Only on weekdays 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- Pet exercising: Allowed in designated area only

Rule 9.2: Common Hall Booking
Implemented: 1st April 2022 (Approved in 33rd AGM held on 20th March 2022)
- Hall available for members' events only
- Booking advance notice: 3 days minimum
- Booking fee: Rs 2000 per event (6 hours)
- Extended time: Rs 300 per additional hour
- Security deposit: Rs 5000 (refundable after event)
- Cleanup responsibility: Organizer must clean within 2 hours

Rule 9.3: Terrace and Rooftop Area
Implemented: 15th March 2021 (Approved in 32nd AGM held on 28th February 2021)
- Terrace: Restricted to maintenance staff only
- No unauthorized persons allowed on terrace
- Water tank area: Off-limits to all except authorized staff
- Solar panel area: Off-limits (being maintained)
- Event organization: Approval from Management Committee required

Rule 9.4: Staircase and Common Corridors
Implemented: 1st June 2020 (Approved in 31st AGM held on 18th May 2020)
- No obstruction in staircases or corridors
- No storage of goods in common areas
- No laundry hanging in corridors
- No advertisement posters on walls
- Cleanliness: Staff cleans daily 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM

10. PENALTIES AND FINES
========================================================

Rule 10.1: Fine Categories
Implemented: 1st January 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- Administrative fine: Rs 100-500 (minor violations)
- Operational fine: Rs 500-1000 (moderate violations)
- Serious fine: Rs 1000-5000 (serious violations)
- Legal action: For repeated violations and serious offenses

Rule 10.2: Fine Payment
- Fine due within 15 days of issuance
- Late payment fine: Rs 50 per day after 15 days
- If not paid within 30 days: Case escalated to management committee
- Fine non-payment: No new certifications issued (for bank loans, etc.)

Rule 10.3: Fine Waiver and Appeals
- Appeal must be filed within 30 days of fine issuance
- Appeal review by Grievance Redressal Committee
- Waiver: Only for genuine hardship cases (requires documentation)
- First-time violators: 50% discount on fine (at committee discretion)

11. FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT
========================================================

Rule 11.1: Payment Methods and Channels
Implemented: 1st January 2023 (Approved in 34th AGM held on 15th December 2022)
- Payments accepted: Cash, Cheque, Online transfer, Mobile wallet
- Bank details: Ashirwad Society Account, Bank Name: ICICI Bank, Account: 1234567890
- IFSC Code: ICIC0000123
- Payment receipt: Issued within 24 hours
- Non-clearance of cheque: Rs 500 fine

Rule 11.2: Financial Year and Audit
- Financial year: April to March
- Monthly accounts closure: 10th of every month
- Quarterly audit: By internal auditor
- Annual audit: By chartered accountant (March 31st)
- Annual financial statement: Presented in AGM (April)

Rule 11.3: Arrears and Recovery
- Arrears notification: Sent on 15th of following month
- Show cause notice: If arrears unpaid for 3 months
- Legal action: After 6 months of non-payment
- Lien on flat: Possible after 12 months of arrears
- Eviction notice: Issued after legal proceedings

12. AMENDMENT PROCEDURES
========================================================

Rule 12.1: Rule Amendment Process
- Any member can propose amendment
- Proposal submitted to Management Committee in writing
- Committee reviews and forwards to general body
- Amendment discussed in AGM with 2/3rd majority voting
- Passed amendments effective from next day
- Members informed within 7 days

Rule 12.2: AGM Schedule and Voting
- Annual General Meeting: Third Saturday of April
- Special General Meeting: As needed (notice 15 days in advance)
- Quorum: Minimum 50% members present
- Voting: Simple majority for general matters, 2/3rd for major changes
- Proxy voting: Allowed with written authorization
