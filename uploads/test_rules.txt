SOCIETY RULES AND REGULATIONS

1. PARKING RULES
- Each flat gets 1 reserved parking spot
- Guest parking available in Zone B
- Parking fee: Rs. 500 per month
- No parking in common areas
- Vehicles must be registered

2. MAINTENANCE AND COMMON AREAS
- Monthly maintenance fee: Rs. 2000 per flat
- Maintenance includes water, electricity in common areas
- Common areas: Garden, lobby, parking lot
- Cleanliness to be maintained by all members

3. WATER USAGE
- Water charges: Rs. 100 per 1000 liters
- No water wastage allowed
- Residents responsible for pipeline leaks

4. ELECTRICITY
- Electricity charges: Rs. 8 per unit
- Power cuts managed by administrator
- Backup generator available for emergencies

5. SECURITY AND SAFETY
- Security personnel available 24/7
- All visitors must register at gate
- CCTV surveillance in common areas
- Security deposit: Rs. 10,000 (refundable)

6. VISITING HOURS
- Guest visiting time: 6 AM to 10 PM
- Late night visitors need prior permission
- No outside food delivery after 11 PM

7. COMPLAINTS AND GRIEVANCES
- Complaints to be filed with administrator
- Resolution time: 7 days
- Emergency repairs: 24 hours

8. RULES FOR CHILDREN AND PETS
- Children must be supervised in common areas
- Pets allowed with permission
- Pet owners responsible for cleanliness

9. NOISE RESTRICTIONS
- No loud music after 10 PM
- No construction work on Sundays
- Silence hours: 10 PM to 6 AM

10. AMENITIES AND FACILITIES
- Gym available 6 AM to 9 PM
- Library open during office hours
- Swimming pool (seasonal)
- Community center for events
